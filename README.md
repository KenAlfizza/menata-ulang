# Menata Ulang
A space to cultivate self-compassion and self-acceptance through evidence-based scientific reflection and community, centered around the unconditional acceptance of self. Menata Ulang aims to increase individual awareness of behaviors, thoughts, and feelings that impact life experiences and perspectives. 
Menata Ulang will serve as a community that accommodates the journey to discover, heal, and rebuild ourselves.Menata Ulang provides a space for discussion, listening, and reflecting on personal experiences and identity. It also delivers skills to manage semi-crisis mental health situations and resources for critical mental health emergencies. 
Furthermore, Menata Ulang is envisioned as a place for friends to grow and learn from their traumatic experiences. We hope that friends at Menata Ulang can love, accept, and navigate themselves after understanding both the worst and best parts of who they are.

# Website
Members of Menata Ulang can access website features designed to support and accompany them on their journey as mental health warriors. In line with the vision of Menata Ulang, users can access evidence-based scientific information about mental illnesses, fictional stories themed around mental health, and more.

# Development
To build a reliable and safe space for our community, the digital backbone of Menata Ulang is crafted with modern, cutting-edge technology.
- Next.js Frontend: Provides a beautiful, fast, and responsive user interface across all mobile and desktop devices.
- Deno Backend: Ensures a highly secure, privacy-focused environment to protect all user interactions and community data.
